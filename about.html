About ITPC

ITPC is the IT Program Council of De La Salle University – Dasmariñas (DLSU-D). The council runs events, workshops and activities for its members, and looks after the materials and officers that make them happen.

What this system does

The ITPC Program Council ERP keeps the council's day-to-day records in one place, so nothing lives in scattered chats and spreadsheets.

  • Inventory Track merchandise, event materials and office supplies, with low-stock alerts.
  • Officers See every officer, their position, committee and active status.
  • Events Plan meetings, workshops, seminars and socials on a shared calendar.
  • Change log A record of who changed what, and when, across the whole system.
  • Reports & backups Print a status report or download a backup of all the data.
